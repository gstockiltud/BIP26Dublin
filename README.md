# STMP SENSATE (Dublin 2026)

### `stmp26` `stmp26dublin` `sensate` `upv` `utcluj` `h-da` `unicas` `fh-campus-wien` `tallaght` `tu-dublin` `eut-plus` `tokyo-electron` `tel` `osnma` `esa` `galileo` `short-term-mobility` `erasmus-plus` `esp32-s3` `embedded-systems`

> **Important Notice on Program Nomenclature** > *In alignment with European funding and administrative guidelines, the official designation for this year's initiative has **changed from BIP (Blended Intensive Program) to STMP (Short Term Mobility Program)**.*

This repository hosts technical documentation supporting the **SENSATE** international summer school, run under the **Short Term Mobility Program (STMP)** in Dublin from June 29th to July 4th, 2026. **SENSATE** stands for **SENsors and Software for Applications and Technological Education**. 

Ultimately, this was an event entirely for the students (over 55 of them from 6 countries: Austria, Germany, Italy, Romania, Spain, and Ireland), and it was their dedication, brilliance, and hard work that made it a resounding success. Many of the teams created their own GitHub repositories. A special thanks to Dr. Deirdre McQuillan, Principal Representative, European University of Technology, and all her team at EUT+ for making this possible (see [EUT+ Alliance Website](https://www.univ-tech.eu/) and the [TU Dublin EUT+ Hub](https://www.tudublin.ie/explore/about-the-university/european-university-of-technology/)), and to all partner institutes inside and outside EUT+.

<details>
<summary><b>4. Celebrating Our Outstanding Students (Click to expand)</b></summary>

A massive word of praise and thanks goes out to all the participating students from diverse disciplines related in one way or another to engineering and problem solving. You brought incredible energy, outstanding teamwork, and good humor to the labs—proudly representing your families, universities, and countries. Throughout this intensive week, you rapidly mastered and deployed a wide array of advanced technical and professional skills:

* **Hardware & Software Prototyping:** Working directly with ESP32-S3 microcontrollers and the Arduino IDE with libraries for GNSS and TFT displays. You used AI in creative ways, showing great initiative to assist in building heat maps, tracking paths, a 3D campus relief diagram, advanced dashboards, and signal quality metrics. Your work showcased excellent web design with SQL support, and many teams went above and beyond by creating interactive games which were successfully debuted on the Tallaght Campus.
* **Advanced Satellite Navigation:** Using NMEA protocols, including Galileo OSNMA authentication and anti-spoofing techniques—an area of technology where the European Space Agency (ESA) currently leads the world.
* **Rapid Problem Solving:** Debugging complex firmware, handling physical sensor calibration, and utilizing AI-assisted workflows for swift hardware diagnostic refactoring, while making clever use of 3D printing and the gamification of measurements.
* **Technical Presentation:** Communicating intricate engineering concepts clearly in very high-quality video presentations, where the absolute strongest aspect demonstrated across all groups was your phenomenal teamwork.
</details>
